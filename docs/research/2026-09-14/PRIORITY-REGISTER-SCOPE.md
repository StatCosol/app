# Priority register scope

User priority confirmed: 14 September 2026.

Focus on Telangana, Andhra Pradesh, Karnataka, Tamil Nadu, Kerala, Maharashtra, Haryana, Gujarat, Odisha, Madhya Pradesh, Delhi and Central Government jurisdiction. Cover applicable Labour Acts as well as all four Labour Codes. Existing work for other jurisdictions remains available, but additional expansion there is secondary to closing this scope.

## Act-by-Act coverage checklist

For each jurisdiction, inventory the following families wherever applicable. A family is not automatically an obligation for every branch.

1. Code on Wages and the associated state/Central rules; legacy minimum wages, payment of wages, bonus and equal-remuneration registers where saved or needed historically.
2. OSH Code and rules; legacy factories, contract labour, inter-state migrant workers, construction, motor transport, plantations, mines, beedi/cigar and other covered sector records, with period-specific supersession/savings checks.
3. Social Security Code, rules and schemes; EPF, ESI, maternity, gratuity, employees compensation and construction cess records. Distinguish employee records, contributions, event records and authority registers.
4. Industrial Relations Code and rules; employer records under applicable standing-orders/industrial-dispute arrangements, with trade-union and authority-owned records classified separately.
5. State Shops and Establishments Acts and rules, including worker, attendance, wages, leave, overtime and other specifically prescribed registers.
6. Labour Welfare Fund Acts/rules and prescribed fines, unpaid accumulation, contribution and related records.
7. National/festival holiday and state holiday legislation; distinguish registers from notices, statements and returns.
8. Professional tax and state labour-related welfare schemes where they impose relevant establishment records or returns.
9. Apprentices, child/adolescent labour and other applicable workforce/sector requirements. Do not invent a register where the law requires only a notice, policy, application or return.

## Register mapping and acceptance

Each record must have jurisdiction, appropriate government, Act/Code, rules, rule reference, exact form number, title, source notification, publication/effective dates, lifecycle status, ownership, applicable establishment/workforce, usage and retention requirements.

Lifecycle review distinguishes current rules, saved transitional rules, superseded historical versions and drafts. A draft is not an operational substitute. A Labour Code commencement date alone does not establish the status of every state rule or repeal a separate state Act.

Generation must follow the selected Act and branch applicability. Principal-employer, contractor and employee responsibilities remain separate. Use approved enrollment, attendance and payroll data only where the prescribed field actually represents that data. Incident, leave, maternity, welfare and other non-payroll records need their respective evidence.

Reuse one underlying record only when the applicable instrument permits it, with the legal relationship and scope recorded. Same names or form numbers do not establish equivalence. Andhra Pradesh and Telangana bindings remain separate even where a layout is identical.

Completion for each form requires primary-source verification, all prescribed fields/annexures, applicable period and ownership checks, data mapping, validation, approval/version history and a tested printed sample. Language and authentication requirements remain explicit. Do not label a state complete merely because a wage register works.

## First source check

The [Telangana government integrated-register order, G.O.Ms.No.6 dated 2 March 2019](https://ipass.telangana.gov.in/viewpdf.aspx?filepathnew=D%3A%2FTS-iPASSFinal%2Fdocs%2F2019LETF_MS6+%282%29.PDF) requires Forms II and III together and separately prescribes the annual return. Its current scope must be reconciled with later rules and savings before operational activation. This is not evidence that AP and Telangana have identical current Code formats.
